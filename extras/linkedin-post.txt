POSTING TIPS (2 minutes, worth it)
----------------------------------------
1. Post on a weekday between 9–11am (Bangladesh time) — best reach.
2. Post it as your own status, not a reshare, so the link preview shows.
3. In the same post, tag Whizz People (type @Whizz People) — their page may reshare.
4. After posting, go to your profile → Featured → Add a link → paste the portfolio URL.
5. Also add the URL in Contact info → Website (label: Portfolio).
6. Reply to every comment in the first hour — LinkedIn boosts early engagement.
