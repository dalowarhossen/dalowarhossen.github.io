01About

I'm Dalowar — an SEO Executive at Whizz People, a London-founded digital agency working across search, web and paid media.

My work sits where research meets execution: finding the queries worth ranking for, fixing the pages that should rank for them, and earning links that make those pages credible. Day to day that means keyword research, on-page optimisation, outreach and reporting — plus the unglamorous data work that keeps a campaign honest.

04Approach

Four steps. No mystery.

01

Audit & baseline

Before changing anything I record where the site stands: indexation, rankings, traffic, backlinks. No baseline means no honest report later.

02

Prioritise by impact

A short list ranked by effort against likely impact. Most sites don't need eighty fixes; they need the right five, in the right order.

03

Ship the work

Keywords mapped, pages optimised, outreach sent, tracking updated. Work happens in the tools your team already uses.

04

Measure & explain

A monthly readout of what moved, what didn't, and what to decide next. If something isn't working, you'll hear it from me first.
